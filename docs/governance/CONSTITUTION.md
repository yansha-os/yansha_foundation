# Yansha Islamic Software Constitution & Non-Negotiable Directives

## 1. Core Quran & Hifz Are Free Forever
Reading, listening, memorization, revision, mistake detection, Tajweed learning, and progress tracking shall never be gated behind a paywall, subscription, or token gate.

## 2. No Paywall for Sacred Knowledge
Financial status must never be a barrier to memorizing or understanding the Holy Quran.

## 3. Open-Source Transparency & Reproducibility
The codebase, alignment algorithms, phonetic distance matrices, and scoring calculations are fully inspectable, auditable, and open for community review.

## 4. Sacred Data Integrity & Source Attribution
All Arabic text (Uthmani, Simple, IndoPak), translations, and Tafsir must be attributed to verified canonical authorities (e.g. King Fahd Complex, Tanzil, Quran.com, Tarteel QUL).

## 5. Teacher-Respectful Humility
The AI system provides diagnostic suggestions and advisory notes ("Possible issue detected"). It explicitly acknowledges its limitations and does not claim to replace a qualified human Shaykh or Mu'allim.

## 6. Offline-First Autonomy
The application must operate without perpetual cloud dependency. Core text, caching, and client-side neural inference (WebGPU/WASM) must function in offline or low-connectivity environments.

## 7. Zero Distraction, Zero Ads
No commercial advertisements, popups, or sponsored distractions shall ever appear inside Quranic reading or recitation workflows.

## 8. Absolute Voice Privacy
User recitation audio is processed locally on-device by default. Voice recordings are never sold, monetized, or harvested for commercial advertising.

## 9. Multilingual & Universal Accessibility
The interface and educational ladders must support global seekers regardless of native tongue, reading level, or technical background.

## 10. Canonical Classical Pedagogy
Adherence to traditional Hafs 'an 'Asim Tajweed rules, the 17-lesson Noorani Qaida progression, and the classical 3-pillar Hifz methodology (Sabaq, Sabqi, Manzil).

## 11. Obligatory Worship Is Never Adapted
Automated systems may adapt only *voluntary* practice (Manzil review volume, dhikr targets, optional reading time). No heuristic, rule, recommendation, or cross-module adaptation may ever alter obligatory prayer (Salah) or Zakat — not the schedule, the target, the reminder, nor the calculation. Fiqh obligations are not heuristics.

## 12. Prayer Data Is Never Shared
A user's prayer record is never a shareable scope to a circle, teacher, guild, or leaderboard, at any setting. Sharing exists only for practice a student explicitly asked to be taught (e.g. assigned Hifz progress).
